## 91. Food Truck Daily Prep & Waste Log

Tracks batch quantities made vs. sold at each location.

- Buyer: Food truck owner
- Release wave: 2 (Expand)
- Proposed access: Team workspace
- Runtime AI in the brief: No model required

**Build prompt**

Build “Food Truck Daily Prep & Waste Log” on NeoApps for a food truck owner. Compare prep, sales, waste and remaining portions by batch and location. Limit records and exported data to the owning team. Include a demo with fictional records.

**Acceptance test**

Start with 50 portions, sell 37 and record 5 wasted. Eight remain at that location.
