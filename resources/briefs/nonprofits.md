## Grantmaking & Fundraising

### 101. Grant Application Portal

Collect conditional applications and supporting documents, then give committee reviewers a shared scoring rubric.

- Buyer: Grants officer
- Release wave: 2 (Expand)
- Proposed access: Public submission
- Runtime AI in the brief: No model required

[NeoApps.AI grant application template](https://neoapps.ai/apps/grant-application-portal/)

**Roles and permissions**

Applicants can edit their own drafts and see their decision. Assigned reviewers can score an application. Only the grants officer can issue the decision.

**Build prompt**

Build “Grant Application Portal” on NeoApps for a nonprofit. Ask for a project budget on capital requests and a delivery plan on program requests. Track submitted, under review and decided states. Keep individual scores private. Applicants can edit their own drafts and see their decision. Assigned reviewers can score an application. Only the grants officer can issue the decision. Use fictional records for the demo.

**Acceptance test**

A capital request without a budget stays in draft. After submission, two assigned reviewers score it 3 and 5 out of 5. The officer sees an average of 4 and releases the decision. Another applicant cannot see the file.
