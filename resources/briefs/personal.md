## 1. Weekend Trip Expense Splitter

Tracks shared Airbnb, gas, and dinner receipts with custom split rules for a 6-person trip.

- Buyer: Trip organizer
- Release wave: 1 (Pilot)
- Proposed access: Invited group
- Runtime AI in the brief: No model required

**Build prompt**

Build “Weekend Trip Expense Splitter” on NeoApps for a trip organizer. Split each receipt among selected travelers, attach a photo and show who owes whom. Use invitations and scope records to this group. Include a demo with fictional records.

**Acceptance test**
