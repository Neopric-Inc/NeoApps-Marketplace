## 76. Hackathon Team Finder & Idea Pitch Board

Lets participants post project ideas and recruit teammates.

- Buyer: Hackathon organizer
- Release wave: 2 (Expand)
- Proposed access: Invited group
- Runtime AI in the brief: No model required

**Build prompt**

Build “Hackathon Team Finder & Idea Pitch Board” on NeoApps for a hackathon organizer. Let participants pitch ideas, list skills sought and request to join teams with open seats. Use invitations and scope records to this group. Include a demo with fictional records.

**Acceptance test**

Fill the last seat on a three-person team. Further applicants see the team as full.
