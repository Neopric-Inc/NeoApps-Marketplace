## 16. Friday Team Lunch Poll & Order Collector

Collects menu choices and calculates individual Venmo/Interac totals.

- Buyer: Office lunch coordinator
- Release wave: 1 (Pilot)
- Proposed access: Invited respondents
- Runtime AI in the brief: No model required

**Build prompt**

Build “Friday Team Lunch Poll & Order Collector” on NeoApps for an office lunch coordinator. Configure menu prices, tax, spice and dietary fields. Lock orders at a local cutoff and group dishes for the kitchen. Give invited respondents their own response and the organizer an aggregate view. Include a demo with fictional records.

**Acceptance test**

Two $10 dishes at 15% tax total $23. A submission after 11:30 is closed and existing orders remain visible to the organizer.
